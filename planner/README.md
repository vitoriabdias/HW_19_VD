# Brain Blocks

An ADHD-friendly life organizer, built as one HTML file with no build step.

Open `planner/index.html` in a browser to use it. Your lists are saved in that browser, so use the ⚙️ menu to download a backup now and then.

## Blocks

- **To-do list**: filter by Today, This week, This month or Someday, and by Urgent or Not urgent. Tasks are sorted into a type automatically (errands, pets, health, movement, social, admin, home, work), and you can change the type. "Just tell me one thing to do" picks a single task for you.
- **Groceries**: add several items at once with commas, like `milk, 2 avocados, dog food`. Each item finds its aisle on its own. You can filter by store (Safeway, No Frills and others).
- **Clean house**: chores grouped by room. Daily, weekly and monthly chores come back on their own when they're due.
- **Money**: log money in and out. Shows your account balance, a monthly summary and where your spending went.
- **Self-care**: gentle reminders (tub, facial, massage, seeing a friend and more) with a goal for how often, a 7-day dot tracker and an "I did it!" button.

`brain-blocks.html` is the same app without the page wrapper. That's the version published as a Claude artifact, where lists sync to your Claude account.
